How a drive moves through Hiredesk

The same three steps, every cycle, for every company that walks in.

1

Admin opens the drive

The placement cell verifies the company, sets eligibility criteria and opens the drive to the matching batch.

2

Students apply, company shortlists

Eligible students apply from their dashboard; recruiters filter, shortlist and move candidates through rounds.

3

Offers are recorded

Offers are logged against each student's profile and roll up into the admin's placement report automatically.

Why the placement cell runs on Hiredesk

Built for a single institution's placement season — not a generic job board.

01

Role‑based access

Admins, recruiters and students each see only what belongs to them — no shared inboxes or spreadsheets.

02

Eligibility auto‑check

CGPA, backlog and branch criteria are applied automatically before a student can apply to a drive.

03

Live drive calendar

Every test, interview round and result date sits on one calendar visible to whoever needs it.

04

Exportable reports

Branch‑wise and year‑wise placement statistics, ready for NAAC and NBA documentation.
